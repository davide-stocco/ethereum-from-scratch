# Ether, wei and gwei

!!! warning "Stub — not written yet"

    This page is an outline. Delete this box when you fill it in.

**Fork-proof.** 10<sup>18</sup> wei per ether, fixed since genesis.

## Questions this page should answer

- What is ether, and how is it different from "Ethereum"?
- Why is the smallest unit called **wei**, and why are there 10<sup>18</sup> of them in one ether?
- What is **gwei** (10<sup>9</sup> wei), and why do fees get quoted in it rather than ether?
- Why does Ethereum use integers everywhere instead of decimals? (Floating-point rounding errors would break consensus — every node must get byte-identical results.)
- What do the numbers look like in practice? A balance of `2500000000000000000` wei is 2.5 ether.
