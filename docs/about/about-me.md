# Who's writing this

I'm **Davide Stocco**. I'm learning Ethereum, and this site is how I'm doing it.

The approach is deliberate: I don't think you understand something until you can explain it in an order that makes sense to someone who knows nothing. So rather than take notes, I'm writing the explanation I wanted when I started — and when a page is hard to write, that's usually a sign I haven't understood the thing yet.

Which means this site has an obvious caveat: **it's written by a learner, not an expert.** I've tried to be careful, to work from primary sources, and to say plainly when something is beyond what I can explain yet. But if you find a mistake, I'd genuinely rather know. Corrections are the most useful thing you can send me.

**More about me:** <https://sites.google.com/view/davidestocco/>

## The site

Built with [MkDocs](https://www.mkdocs.org/) and [Material](https://squidfunk.github.io/mkdocs-material/). The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/davide-stocco/ethereum-from-scratch).

Two rules shape it: **nothing is explained before its prerequisites**, and the published material is restricted to the parts of Ethereum that have **never changed** — see [how this site handles forks](forks.md) for why that matters.
